Methodology

Each tab is a different launch window. Inside every window the ranking variables, most prioritized to least:

1. 24h trading volume — 70% of the score. Higher volume ranks higher.

2. Liquidity — 30% of the score. More liquidity in the pool ranks higher.

Both are scored on a log scale, then combined. A higher spot means more trading relative to the money sitting in the pool.

Market cap is shown on each card for context. It is not part of the ranking.

24H — pair created in the last 24 hours.

7D — pair created between 1 and 7 days ago.

1M — pair created between 7 and 30 days ago.

The time next to the chain is how long ago the pair was created.

Source: DexScreener. Not a buy signal.
